What's in each report

Five tabs. Your tab choice is saved in the URL, so bookmarks keep your place.

Search All

Search every offer by name, brand or store. Click column headers to sort, use store chips to filter.

All Offers

All food deals grouped by category with price trends. Toggle 🥇 Cheapest only to see the best deal per category.

Non-Food

Household, beauty & electronics deals. Filter by store or price range.

Store Optimizer

Pick the categories you need and see which single store covers the most at the best total, compared with cherry-picking.

Brand Watchlist

Track brands you care about (e.g. "Nutella"). Matching offers appear automatically each week, saved on your device.

Tips

  • Tap + on any offer to add it to your shopping list (cart icon, grouped by store)
  • Green rows = cheapest currently-available deal in that category
  • Moon/sun icon in the header toggles dark mode
